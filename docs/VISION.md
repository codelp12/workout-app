# Vision

## Problem

Fitness apps are specialized and subscription-priced. A runner who also lifts ends up paying for several apps
(~$10/month each, often ~$50/month total), each padded with social features and engagement bloat, with data
siloed across all of them.

## Goal

A single, focused app for tracking all training, built for its owner rather than for engagement metrics.

## In scope (high level)

- Logging workouts across activity types (e.g. runs, strength sessions, other cardio).
- History and progress over time.
- Data export.

## Explicitly out of scope

- Social feeds, sharing-first features, leaderboards.
- Ads, upsells, paywalled features.

## Open questions

- Platform: mobile, web, or both?
- Data sources: manual entry only, or import from wearables / GPS / health platforms?
- Storage: local-first, self-hosted, or cloud?
- Which activity types matter for v1?
